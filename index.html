Questions

Do I need to pay a deposit?

A small deposit secures your slot, especially for bridal and weekend bookings. Lisa will share the details when she confirms your appointment.

Do you offer home service?

Yes. Lisa can come to you for makeup and lashes. A small transport fee may apply depending on your location.

How early should I book for my wedding?

Ideally 2–3 months ahead, with a trial session a few weeks before the big day.

How should I prepare for my appointment?

Come with a clean, moisturised face for makeup, and clean natural lashes with no mascara for lash appointments. Inspiration photos are always welcome!
